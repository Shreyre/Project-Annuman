Beds and staff today

By role only

The bed count closes a stay with no recorded discharge after five days. An attendance mark is questioned only on a busy day with none of that role’s work in the care record. No individuals and no location tracking.

How Anumaan works

The register says it’s on the shelf. The care says otherwise.

When a medicine runs out, patients are still diagnosed. Courses get cut short, substitutes appear, and slips say “not available”. Anumaan reads those traces to find the stock-outs a register misses.
