    Rolling it out Added after the 30 Sep submission

    • One command per stateA state joins the national view with one command, cleanroom onboard <state>, run in its own Google Cloud project. Its care records stay there; only the counts above are shared.
    • About $1.7 a month in total, for all of India’s PHCsNightly compute for all 31,882 PHCs (MoHFW, 2023) and 25 tracer medicines: about 52 vCPU-minutes a night on Cloud Run. Compute only: taking in the feeds, storage and serving are not counted.
    • A four-week pilot on existing feedsIt runs on what a state already records: diagnoses, dispensing and the stock register. PHC staff get no new app. Week 1 set up, week 2 load history, week 3 shadow mode with shelf checks, week 4 score the alarms and switch on.
    • Shared priors for a new stateA new state starts from the national medians under “What states get back” above, instead of waiting a month to learn its own.

    From the README: the cost is scaled up from a synthetic state of 1,500 PHCs timed on one laptop core; the pilot plan is its “Pilot in four weeks”.

    How Anumaan works

    The register says it’s on the shelf. The care says otherwise.

    When a medicine runs out, patients are still diagnosed. Courses get cut short, substitutes appear, and slips say “not available”. Anumaan reads those traces to find the stock-outs a register misses.

    1. Spot a signalStart with the hidden stock-outs, or search any facility and medicine.
    2. Read the evidenceCompare the register with the care, the forecast, beds and staff.
    3. Confirm and actRecord the pharmacist’s shelf check, then approve transfers or escalate.

      A PHC with no computer can still be read. Photograph one day’s OPD register and dispensing register. Gemini reads the pages into the same report a digital PHC sends, and you check it line by line.

      Nothing is applied until you confirm.

      Read test on 8 held-out synthetic PHC-days: Gemini read 650 of 650 register lines right and missed no row. The pages were drawn in a handwriting font, not photographed from real registers; real registers are not tested yet.

      Demo only: use the sample pages or other synthetic pages. Do not upload real registers: photos with patients’ names on them would go to Gemini at Vertex AI’s global location, not an India-region endpoint.
